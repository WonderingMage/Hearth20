# Beta 0.3.21 — campaign items and personal journals

Open Journal → Items & inventory. The DM creates a named item with a PNG and plain-text description, then places it by clicking a map square. Items start concealed in the DM vault. The item inspector or board right-click menu reveals/conceals or stows them; the DM may also give an item directly to a saved player. Players right-click a revealed board item to pick it up. It disappears from the board and becomes a private inventory entry with its image and description. Right-click an owned inventory entry to destroy it, with confirmation.

Journal personal notes save to the host after a short typing pause or Save notes now. The DM can select a saved player's journal to record homebrew information. Other players receive neither that journal nor its inventory. The existing separate DM session notes remain private. Manual save and the single five-minute autosave preserve journals, item data, images and authenticated owners. The same local player identity restores these when reconnecting; display names alone do not prove ownership. Players should keep their existing Hearth20 profile when switching machines or reinstalling. A campaign saved before a change naturally restores that earlier state.

The unused Token Sheet tab is removed. The upcoming portable also includes the previously unreleased wizard avatars, DM-only supplied D20 pendant and two CRT eye treatments. Voice, mouth and head tracking remain cancelled/deferred as directed.

## Data and authority

`player_journals` is an optional campaign dictionary from authenticated player principal to plain text, up to 20,000 characters each. `custom_items` is an optional array of unique records: id, name, description, png_asset_id, holder, scene_id, position and hidden. Empty holder means an item is on the board; `dm-local` means the DM's vault; a player principal means that player's inventory. PNGs live in the existing campaign asset table. Older campaign saves without these fields remain supported.

Only the host creates/places/reveals/stows/gives items. Authenticated clients may update their own notes, claim an actually visible board item or destroy their own inventory entry. Requests never accept a client-supplied owner/role. The host checks map cover, fog/light visibility, the player's token-bound scene and spectating state before pickup; claims change the holder atomically. Repeated or competing pickups fail. A stored token cannot claim a board item, while its existing inventory and notes remain accessible. Destroyed items stay gone in later saves. Unreferenced item PNGs are removed without deleting art used by another item or token.

Player snapshots carry only that recipient's personal notes, inventory and visible board items. Concealed/other-map item names, descriptions, identities and PNGs are excluded. Inventories remain available during map cover/loading; the existing authorized image cache avoids resending unchanged art. Item PNGs are validated with the existing bounded PNG decoder and proportionally reduced to 512 pixels. Names are limited to 80 characters, descriptions to 8,000, campaign items to 256 and each player's inventory to 96. Oversized/malformed requests, full storage and invalid saves fail explicitly. New hosts advertise the feature; a new player connected to an older host sees an update message instead of calling unsupported RPCs.

## Verification and build

`tools/run_source_check.py belongings` checks native UI, actual right-click routing, concealment/fog/cover/token restrictions, competing claims, private projections, save/reload/autosave, malformed requests, ownership, destruction confirmation/cancellation and persistence. `tools/test_belongings_network.py` runs an isolated host plus two authenticated localhost clients, including real RPCs, reconnecting with the same identity and reloading host-written saves. `sidebar_stability`, `autosave`, campaign-save and map-input checks cover adjacent behavior. Exact portable results and artifact hashes are recorded under `qa/items-release` after packaging. No new real-account Steam or browser test is implied.
