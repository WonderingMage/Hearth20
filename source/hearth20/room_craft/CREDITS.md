# Room material provenance

Materials are from the texture packs supplied by the project owner:
40 Free Wood Textures.zip and 50 Free Stylized Wall Textures.zip.
The selected source records and hashes are retained in materials.json.
Albedo comes from the supplied artwork. Auxiliary normal, roughness and
height channels were previously derived in HearthDesigner; they are approximate
surface-detail maps, not independently measured material scans.
No original archive or model was modified.
